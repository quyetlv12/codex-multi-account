# Copy account card titles

## Why
Operators need to copy the displayed account name directly from its card.

## What Changes
- Clicking or keyboard-activating the card title copies its full alias, display name, or fallback email.
- Report clipboard success or failure through existing localized feedback.
- Keep the separate View details action opening the account detail modal.

## Impact
Frontend only. No account data, authentication, routing, or storage changes.
