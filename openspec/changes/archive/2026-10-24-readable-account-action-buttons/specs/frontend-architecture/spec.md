## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Readable account action labels
Account action buttons and the card View details button SHALL display labels at 14px with bold weight. Labels SHALL fit within their buttons on desktop and mobile in light and dark themes. Existing actions, disabled states and visual variants SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Scan account actions
- **WHEN** the operator views account actions on a desktop or mobile screen
- **THEN** labels appear at 14px with weight 700 without clipping or overlap
- **AND** existing actions and disabled states remain available as before
